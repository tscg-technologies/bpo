Evaluation Dimensions

How Quality Is Evaluated Across Operations

Our quality assurance framework assesses frontline interactions across four primary dimensions, ensuring balanced evaluation between procedural compliance and customer satisfaction.

1. Communication & Empathy

Greeting tone, active listening, respectful phrasing, clarity of explanation, and professional closing etiquette.

2. Procedural & SOP Adherence

Correct authentication of client identity, following approved troubleshooting workflows, and avoiding unauthorized promises.

3. Data Accuracy & Note Logging

Thorough CRM disposition tagging, accurate summaries of customer issues, and timely ticket closure.

4. Resolution Effectiveness

Ensuring the root inquiry was addressed on the first attempt without unnecessary repeat transfers.

Governance Loop

The Quality Improvement Lifecycle

How insights collected from routine evaluations translate directly into agent development and process refinement.

1. Sample Auditing

Independent QA analysts randomly select voice calls and written tickets for rubric scoring.

2. Calibration Sessions

Bi-weekly reviews between QA team and client leaders to align interpretation of scoring criteria.

3. 1-on-1 Coaching

Team leaders review recorded sessions with agents to discuss specific procedural opportunities.

4. Knowledge Refinement

Common customer confusions trigger documentation enhancements in the internal knowledge base.
