People Operations Support

Human Resources Outsourcing & Administrative Support

Supporting internal people teams with disciplined HR administration, candidate interview coordination, onboarding paperwork verification, and personnel file management.

Request a Consultation All BPO Services
Service Overview

Operational Context & Purpose

Human Resources teams should focus on organizational culture, talent development, and leadership retention rather than drowning in clerical onboarding forms.

Our HR outsourcing services manage the administrative mechanics of the employee lifecycle, from candidate scheduling to compliance document filing and HR helpdesk support.

Operational Challenges

Business Problems Addressed

Key operational bottlenecks and delivery risks mitigated through this service workflow.

Recruiting Coordination Bottlenecks

Delays in scheduling candidate interviews causing top applicants to accept competing offers.

Fragmented Onboarding Paperwork

New hires frustrated by missing forms, delayed equipment tracking, and disjointed welcome steps.

Personnel Record Inaccuracies

Disorganized employee records creating compliance exposure during audits.

Repetitive HR Policy Inquiries

HR leaders spending hours answering routine questions regarding holiday policies and benefits forms.

Service Scope

Detailed Operational Scope

Specific tasks, procedural duties, and administrative checkpoints included within this delivery model.

Interview Coordination

Liaising between recruiters, hiring managers, and candidates to schedule multi-stage interviews.

Onboarding Document Verification

Collecting and checking identity documents, tax forms, and direct deposit details.

HRIS Database Maintenance

Entering new hire records, title changes, department transfers, and terminations into HRIS platforms.

Internal HR Helpdesk Support

Answering employee questions regarding leave balances, policy manuals, and standard forms.

Reference & Background Coordination

Tracking completion of third-party background screenings and reference verifications.

Offboarding Documentation

Compiling exit paperwork, checklists, and equipment return documentation.

Execution Lifecycle

Typical Operating Workflow

Step-by-step procedure governing how incoming tasks are received, verified, executed, and archived.

1

Candidate Stage

Scheduling interviews across time zones and tracking applicant status in ATS.

2

Offer & Intake

Issuing standardized offer paperwork and tracking electronic signatures.

3

Onboarding Checklist

Guiding new hires through document submission and provisioning requests.

4

Record Archival

Creating indexed personnel files in the HRIS adhering to retention standards.

5

Ongoing Support

Assisting employees with routine administrative requests and benefit form questions.

Communication & Tooling

Human Resources Information Systems (BambooHR, Workday, Gusto, ADP paradigms), Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), and secure document portals.

Quality Considerations

Scored on scheduling turnaround speed, document verification thoroughness, data entry accuracy in HRIS, and confidentiality compliance.

Potential Business Benefits

  • Faster Hiring Cycles: Prompt candidate scheduling prevents delays and improves candidate experience scores.
  • Flawless New Hire Onboarding: Structured checklists ensure every employee begins their first day fully documented.
  • Relieved Internal HR Teams: Frees senior HR professionals to focus on strategic organizational development.
