Structured Methodology

How Our Operational Process Works

A five-stage governance model designed to de-risk transitions, clarify SLA metrics, and maintain continuous service quality.

01

Discovery

Understand business objectives, current operating volume, technology landscape, and KPIs.

02

Scope Definition

Map explicit workflows, boundary limits, RACI matrices, and standard operating procedures (SOPs).

03

Resource Planning

Staff allocation, training material development, tooling configurations, and access controls setup.

04

Transition

Pilot execution, shadow testing, SLA baseline validation, and full operational launch.

05

Continuous Improvement

Weekly calibration audits, QA scorecards, root cause analysis, and ongoing process optimization.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Outsourcing

Key considerations for organizations exploring business process outsourcing partnerships.

Business Process Outsourcing involves delegating specific non-core operational functions—such as customer contact handling, technical troubleshooting, data entry, or invoice processing—to specialized service teams who execute the workflows according to agreed service standards.

Voice BPO encompasses spoken telephone communication, such as inbound customer support, technical assistance helplines, and verified follow-up calls. Non-voice BPO encompasses written and back-office channels, including email support, live web chat, data processing, catalog moderation, and transaction filing.

Quality management typically includes standardized call scoring rubrics, ticket QA checks, sample validation audits, SLA compliance monitoring, and periodic calibration sessions with client stakeholders to maintain consistent accuracy.
