Digital Record Archival

Document Management & Digitization Services

Transforming unstructured physical and digital files into organized, searchable, and securely indexed digital document repositories.

Request a Consultation All BPO Services
Service Overview

Operational Context & Purpose

Organizations produce vast quantities of contracts, invoices, compliance forms, and historical records that become liabilities when unsearchable or disorganized.

Our document management workflows structure, index, and audit digital assets to ensure rapid information retrieval, compliance retention, and clean organizational archives.

Operational Challenges

Business Problems Addressed

Key operational bottlenecks and delivery risks mitigated through this service workflow.

Lost or Unsearchable Records

Staff spending hours locating critical historical contracts or customer paperwork.

Unverified OCR Extractions

Optical Character Recognition software generating gibberish on faint or tilted scans.

Regulatory Non-Compliance

Failure to observe mandatory document retention or destruction schedules.

Inconsistent Naming Standards

Disparate naming conventions preventing automated filing across departments.

Service Scope

Detailed Operational Scope

Specific tasks, procedural duties, and administrative checkpoints included within this delivery model.

Document Scanning Review

Inspecting incoming scans for completeness, correct orientation, and page clarity.

OCR Verification & Correction

Comparing automated OCR output against source documents to fix textual misreads.

Metadata Indexing & Tagging

Applying document dates, entity names, reference numbers, and custom taxonomy tags.

Structured Folder Organization

Filing documents into standardized, hierarchical cloud or on-premise repositories.

Redaction & Masking

Blacking out sensitive social security numbers, banking digits, or PII before archival.

Retention & Archival Management

Categorizing records by required retention timelines and flagging expired files.

Execution Lifecycle

Typical Operating Workflow

Step-by-step procedure governing how incoming tasks are received, verified, executed, and archived.

1

File Ingestion

Receiving batch PDFs, TIFFs, or scanned document bundles.

2

Pre-Processing Audit

Checking image clarity, de-skewing, and verifying all pages are present.

3

OCR & Metadata Tagging

Extracting key field data and applying standardized file naming conventions.

4

Quality Audit

Reviewing sample indexed files to confirm naming and tagging accuracy.

5

Repository Upload

Publishing indexed records to the designated Document Management System (DMS).

Communication & Tooling

Enterprise Document Management Systems (SharePoint, OpenText, Box, Google Drive enterprise), OCR software, and PDF manipulation suites.

Quality Considerations

Scored on metadata tagging accuracy, file naming compliance, image legibility verification, and adherence to retention schedules.

Potential Business Benefits

  • Instant Document Retrieval: Standardized metadata allows employees to locate any contract or invoice in seconds.
  • Reduced Physical Storage Costs: Digitizing and indexing eliminates the need for expensive physical document warehouses.
  • Enhanced Audit Readiness: Consolidated digital archives simplify preparation for external financial and operational audits.
